Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are

Loopdesk is a software company based in the United Arab Emirates. Contact: hello@getloopdesk.com.

Our role

Businesses such as beauty centres and clinics use Loopdesk to answer and book their own clients. For client messages and bookings, the business decides what is collected and why, and we process that data on its behalf and on its instructions. For the owners and staff who use our service, we are responsible for their account data.

What we process

  • Business details: name, address, services, prices, opening hours, staff names and working times.
  • Client details sent by the business's clients: name, phone number, messages, booking history and stated preferences.
  • Consent records: when a client agreed to receive messages and when they asked to stop.
  • Technical data: message delivery status and service logs.

Booking an appointment does not need medical records. We ask clinics not to send them through Loopdesk.

Why we process it

  • To reply to clients, book, change and cancel appointments, and send reminders.
  • To keep the business's diary and send the owner a summary.
  • To keep the service secure, fix faults and improve replies.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use client data for our own marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms, through the WhatsApp Business Platform, to send and receive messages.
  • Our hosting and AI providers, under contract and only to run the service.
  • Authorities, where UAE law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep client data for as long as the business uses Loopdesk, then delete it within 90 days of the end of the contract, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your choices

Clients can reply STOP at any time to stop messages. To see, correct or delete your data, contact the business or email hello@getloopdesk.com and we will help within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited to people who need it to run the service.

Law

We follow UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data. The service is not meant for people under 18 to sign up to.

Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the date above.
