Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

  • Beauty centres, clinics and other appointment businesses use Loopdesk to answer you, book you and remind you on their own WhatsApp number, and on their Instagram account and Facebook Page. The business decides what it keeps about you, and we look after it on the business's behalf.
  • We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.
  • At beauty centres, replies are written with the help of AI. At dental clinics, your data is stored in the UAE and your chat is not sent to any AI service.
  • Reply STOP at any time and the messages stop. Your appointments stay exactly as they are.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, email hello@getloopdesk.com.

1. Who we are and our role

Loopdesk ("we", "us") makes software for businesses that work by appointment, such as beauty centres, clinics and barbershops. Loopdesk is operated by LOOP DESK SOLUTIONS - FZCO, Service Licence no. 94491 issued by the Dubai Integrated Economic Zones Authority, with its registered address at DSO-THUB-G-D-FLEX-G039E, THUB, Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

When you message one of these businesses through Loopdesk, that business is in charge of your data (under the law it is the "controller"). We process your data for the business and only on its instructions (we are its "processor").

Dental clinics are licensed healthcare providers. When you message a clinic, the clinic is in charge of your data as part of your care, and we handle it for the clinic under the UAE health data law (see section 11).

For the owners and staff who sign in to Loopdesk, we are in charge of their account data.

2. What we collect

If you are a client of a business

  • Your phone number, and your name as it shows on WhatsApp or as you tell us.
  • If you message a business on Instagram or Facebook Messenger: the ID Meta gives your account for that business, your name as Meta shares it, and what you type to book (such as your mobile, name and date of birth). We don't read your profile, posts, photos or followers.
  • Your messages to the business and the replies you receive.
  • Your appointments: what you booked, when, with whom, and any change or cancellation.
  • Preferences you share, such as who you like to see or the times that suit you.
  • Your message choices: when you agreed to receive messages, and when you asked to stop.

If you own or work at a business

  • What you sign in with: your phone number, or your email and password. We send one-time sign-in codes on WhatsApp, and store passwords only in scrambled form.
  • Business details: name, address, services, prices, opening hours, team names and working hours.
  • Your settings, such as how you would like Loopdesk to address you.

If you ask us to call you

  • What you type in the call-back form on our website: your name, business name, mobile, email, type of business, emirate, number of branches and the best time to call.
  • Whether you ticked a box to hear from us by email or on WhatsApp, and when.

If your business is publicly listed

  • To find businesses we could help, our team looks up beauty businesses in the UAE by hand, on Google Maps and on public booking websites. We note the business name, area, map location, its public business phone number and which booking platforms it is listed on.
  • We only use public business details, never the personal number of an owner or staff member. We tell you where we found your details the first time we contact you. If you ask us not to contact you, we erase the phone number and keep only a note with a scrambled code made from it. The number can't be read back from the code, but it lets us recognise your number so we never contact you by mistake.

Technical data

Message delivery status and the service logs we need to keep Loopdesk secure and working.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to you, book, change and cancel appointments, and send reminders.
  • To invite you back at the right time. If you agreed to hear from the business, we use your past visits (for example, when you last had a treatment) to send a friendly note when your usual time comes round. You can turn these off at any time.
  • To send the owner a weekly summary of bookings and returning clients.
  • To sign owners and staff in and keep their accounts safe.
  • To call you back when you ask us to on our website. We send you ideas, tips and offers only if you ticked a box for it, and you can stop them at any time.
  • To keep the service secure, fix faults and improve the quality of replies.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising. We never use one business's client data for another business.

4. AI

At dental clinics, messages to patients use set wording and buttons. Your chat is not sent to any AI service.

At beauty centres, chat replies are written with the help of Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. To write a reply, we send it the conversation and the booking details it needs. Anthropic handles this data under terms that do not allow it to train its models on it.

The beauty centre's own front-desk team can log a booking by sending a voice note, which may include your name, number and the appointment you want. To turn it into text, we send that voice note to Gemini, an AI model made by Google. Google works for us as a service provider and may not use it to train its models. We do not keep the recording. Your own voice notes to the business are never sent to Google.

The demo on our website uses AI with a sample clinic and made-up patients only.

The AI writes replies and arranges appointments. It does not make decisions about you that have legal or similar serious effects, and it never gives medical advice: questions about your health go to the clinic's team.

5. WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Meta

Messages between you and the business travel through the WhatsApp Business Platform, run by Meta. Your use of WhatsApp itself is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

A business can also connect its Instagram account and Facebook Page, so that Loopdesk answers the messages people send it there. Business owners give Loopdesk this permission through Facebook, and can take it back at any time from Loopdesk or from their Facebook settings. We use it only to read and answer messages sent to that business, never to post or to read anything else. Your use of Instagram and Facebook is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

6. Messages and STOP

  • A business only sends you messages if you contacted it, booked with it, or agreed to hear from it.
  • Reply STOP (or إيقاف) at any time and all messages stop at once. Stopping messages never cancels your appointment.
  • Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Confirmations and reminders for an appointment you booked are part of the booking. Notes inviting you back, offers, and asks to leave a review or follow the business on Instagram are only sent if you agreed to receive them.
  • Reviews are never rewarded.

7. Who we share it with

  • The business you are dealing with.
  • Meta, to carry messages over WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.
  • Anthropic, to help write replies at beauty centres. Dental clinic patients' data is never sent to Anthropic.
  • Google, to turn beauty centre front-desk voice notes into text.
  • Our cloud hosting providers, to store data and run the service. Our main servers and database are on Microsoft Azure in the UAE.
  • The clinic's practice-management system, where your booking is saved. The clinic chooses this system and its provider.
  • Our email provider, to deliver call-back requests to our team and to send emails.
  • Government authorities, only where UAE law requires it.

Each provider works under a contract and may use the data only to run the service.

8. Data outside the UAE

Dental clinic patients: your data is stored on our servers in the UAE (Microsoft Azure, UAE North), and backups stay in the same region. It is not sent to any AI service. Two things sit outside our servers. Messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger, which may carry them abroad, so we keep medical details out of messages. And your booking is saved in the clinic's own practice-management system, which the clinic chooses and is responsible for.

Everyone else: some of our providers handle data outside the United Arab Emirates, for example in the United States. When they do, we rely on the contracts and safeguards that UAE data protection law accepts.

9. How long we keep it

  • Dental clinic chats: deleted automatically 8 days after the last message. Your bookings stay in the clinic's own system, under the clinic's rules for patient records.
  • Everything else a business keeps with us (at beauty centres this includes chats, bookings and visit history): while the business uses Loopdesk. We delete it within 90 days after the business stops, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • If you ask us to delete your data: we delete it within 30 days. We keep only a note that you asked us to stop messaging you, so we never contact you again by mistake.
  • Owner sign-in codes: expire after 10 minutes, and we only store them in scrambled form. Signed-in sessions end after 30 days.
  • Call-back requests: if we don't end up working together, we delete them within 12 months. We keep only a note if you asked us to stop contacting you.
  • Public business listings we noted: until we no longer need them to find businesses to work with. If you ask us not to contact you, we erase the phone number straight away and keep only the do-not-contact note and its scrambled code.
  • Service logs: up to 90 days.

10. Your rights, and deleting your data

Under UAE law you can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you, or give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it, or stop messaging you;
  • withdraw any consent you gave.

To delete your data, email hello@getloopdesk.com with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us which business you messaged and how: your WhatsApp number, or your Instagram or Facebook name. We delete your messages, bookings we hold and the IDs Meta gave us within 30 days, and confirm by email. Appointments already in the business's own diary stay with the business: ask it directly to remove them.

For anything else, email hello@getloopdesk.com or ask the business directly. We reply within 30 days. To protect you, we may ask you to message us from the phone number your request is about. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

11. Health information and dental clinics

Health data in the UAE is protected by Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on the use of information and communication technology in health fields. For dental clinics, we:

  • store patient data in the UAE, and run our servers there;
  • use it only to book, confirm, change and remind, never for medical advice or diagnosis;
  • keep chats for no more than 8 days after the last message;
  • let only the clinic's own team see its patients, each person at the access level the clinic sets.

Messages may come from the clinic's automated assistant. Someone from the clinic's team can step in at any time: just ask.

Booking an appointment never needs medical information. Please do not send medical records, test results or diagnoses in the chat. We ask clinics not to send them either. Any question about your health is answered by the clinic's team.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only the people who need it to run the service can reach it, and every business can only see its own clients.

13. Children

Loopdesk accounts are for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to book for you.

14. The law

We follow the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and, for health data, Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on the use of information and communication technology in health fields, together with the rules of the UAE health authorities (DHA, DOH and MOHAP).

If we change this policy, we post the new version here and update the date at the top.

في عيادات الأسنان، تصل الرسائل إلى المرضى بصياغة ثابتة مع أزرار للاختيار، ولا تُرسل محادثتكم إلى أيّ خدمة ذكاء اصطناعي.

يمكن لفريق الاستقبال في مركز التجميل تسجيل الحجز برسالة صوتية، قد تتضمّن اسمكم ورقمكم والموعد الذي تطلبونه. ولتحويلها إلى نصّ، نرسل تلك الرسالة الصوتية إلى Gemini، وهو نموذج ذكاء اصطناعي من شركة Google. وتعمل Google لصالحنا بصفتها مزوّد خدمة، ولا يحقّ لها استخدامها في تدريب نماذجها. ولا نحتفظ بالتسجيل. أمّا رسائلكم الصوتية أنتم إلى مقدّم الخدمة فلا تُرسل إلى Google أبدًا.

يستخدم العرض التجريبي على موقعنا الذكاء الاصطناعي مع عيادة نموذجية ومرضى افتراضيين فقط.

مرضى عيادات الأسنان: تُحفظ بياناتكم على خوادمنا داخل الدولة (Microsoft Azure، منطقة UAE North)، وتبقى النسخ الاحتياطية في المنطقة نفسها، ولا تُرسل إلى أيّ خدمة ذكاء اصطناعي. ويبقى أمران خارج خوادمنا. الأوّل الرسائل نفسها، إذ تنقلها Meta عبر WhatsApp أو Instagram أو Messenger وقد تمرّ خارج الدولة، ولذلك لا نضمّن رسائلنا تفاصيل طبية. والثاني حجزكم، إذ يُحفظ في نظام إدارة العيادة الذي تختاره العيادة وتتحمّل مسؤوليته.

11. المعلومات الصحية وعيادات الأسنان

يحمي البيانات الصحية في الدولة القانونُ الاتحادي رقم 2 لسنة 2019 في شأن استخدام تقنية المعلومات والاتصالات في المجالات الصحية. ولذلك نلتزم في عيادات الأسنان بما يلي:

  • نحفظ بيانات المرضى داخل الدولة، وتعمل خوادمنا فيها؛
  • نستخدمها للحجز والتأكيد والتعديل والتذكير فقط، لا لنصيحة طبية أو تشخيص؛
